# PulseTrack 💪

**A free workout tracker that lives on your phone and works with no internet.**
Pick a program, log your sets, beat your records. No account, no ads, no sign-up. Your data stays on your phone.

*Version 2.6.4*

PulseTrack installs to your home screen like a normal app. It comes with **21 ready-made programs** (including 12-week plans from Arnold, Ronnie Coleman, Phil Heath and more) and **474 exercises**, and you can add your own of both. It has rest and interval timers, charts, personal records, a 1-rep-max calculator, body tracking, and a muscle map that shows what is recovered and what is behind. You can change how it looks (light or dark, 13 themes, any colour, 8 fonts).

## Install and update

**Install.** Open the app link in your phone's browser once, with internet.

| Phone | Steps |
|---|---|
| iPhone (Safari) | Share button → **Add to Home Screen** → Add |
| Android (Chrome) | ⋮ menu → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**) |

After that it opens full screen like any app and works offline.

**Update.** When new files have been uploaded to GitHub:

1. Open the app **with internet**.
2. Close it fully and open it again.
3. You have the new version, and your data is still there.

Android updates on the first refresh. iPhone can be slower: if you don't see the update, close the app completely and reopen it while online.

## Your first workout

1. Open the app. You land on **Home**. Nothing is picked for you.
2. Tap **Choose a Program** and look around. Opening a program only lets you look, it never makes it yours. Tap **Select** on the one you want (more in [Your current program](#your-current-program)), or start **Freestyle** for a free workout.
3. Tap a day to see every exercise with its sets, reps and rest.
4. For each set, type your **weight** and **reps**, then tap the check mark. The rest timer starts by itself.
5. Tap **Finish Workout**. You get a summary, and the next day lines up as **Up next**.

### Your current program

Your **current program** is the one that Home, the side menu and **Start Workout** use. **You** choose it. The app never picks it for you, and browsing never changes it.

- Every program on the **Programs** list has a **Select** button. The one you picked shows a green **✓ Current** tag.
- Open a program you haven't selected and a bar says **Just looking** with a **Select** button. Inside one of its workouts, a small bar says **Not your current program** with **Select it**, which also makes that day your next one.
- While you look around, Home and the side menu keep showing your own program.
- **Jump to week** counts as choosing the program, because you are starting it there.
- **Empty Workout**, **Do Again**, **Freestyle**, and the **Open** buttons after you add or create a program do not change it.
- If nothing is selected and you finish a program's workout, a note offers **Make "…" your current program?**. Nothing happens unless you tap it.
- Deleting a program you were only looking at leaves your current one alone.

### 12-week plans

- Every day is labelled like **Week 4 of 12 · Day 2**.
- The days list has week headers and scrolls to **Up next** for you.
- **Jump to week** lets you start in the middle. Earlier weeks are marked done.
- Rest days are skipped automatically.
- History remembers which week each workout was in.

### Make your own

- **Program builder:** create a program, add days and exercises, set sets, reps and rest.
- **Edit any program:** add, remove, swap or reorder exercises, and change sets and reps.
- **Save a finished workout as a routine** to repeat it.
- **Supersets** show as A1/A2, B1/B2.
- **Share** a program as a link, or **import** one from a link or a `.json` file.

### Reset and restore

- **Clear Progress** puts a day back to exactly the program's original: added exercises and extra sets go, removed ones come back. Your History and records are never deleted.
- **Deleted a built-in program?** **Add Back a Built-in Program** restores it.

## While you train

### Logging a set

- **Set tags:** **R** regular, **W** warm-up, **D** drop set, **F** failure.
- **Previous numbers:** each set shows what you did last time.
- **Dumbbells:** enter the weight of **one** dumbbell. Your volume counts both hands.
- **Bodyweight moves:** pull-ups and dips use your body weight, with a **Weighted / Assisted** switch.
- **Cardio:** log minutes and distance, and see your pace over time.
- **Notes:** add a note to any exercise or day.
- **Swap an exercise:** tap swap and pick a similar one. **★ Best swaps** lists the closest matches first.
- **Form tips:** each exercise card has a **Form tips ›** link with a short checklist (set-up, the movement, common mistakes) and a button to watch a video on YouTube. Nearly every exercise in the library has one.
- **Warm-up generator:** builds warm-up sets for your first lift.
- **Screen stays awake** during a workout, on phones that allow it.
- **Resume:** if you leave the app mid-workout, it picks up where you stopped.

### Rest timer

- Starts by itself when you check off a set (you can turn this off in Settings).
- In a superset it waits until the last exercise of the round. It does not start after your final set, and it uses a short rest after warm-up sets.
- **Smart rest times:** heavy lifts get longer rests and small lifts shorter. You can set your own time for any exercise.
- Sound, vibration and optional voice tell you when rest is over.

### Workout timer (top of the screen)

A timer starts when you open a workout. **Tap it** for the controls:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Pause / Resume** | For a long break or a phone call. Paused time is not counted. |
| **Stop** | Freezes the time. **Finish Workout** then saves that frozen time, even if you finish later. **Start again** continues from where it stopped. |
| **Fix the time** | Type how long it really took, like `75` or `1:15`. |
| **Start over** | Back to 0:00. |

The little pill shows its state: green dot is running, orange bars is paused, grey square is stopped. Past an hour it reads `1:12:06`. It keeps the right time even if you close the app, and it is separate from the rest timer.

Forgot it? If you tap **Finish Workout** with the timer running and you haven't logged a set for 30 minutes or more, the app asks whether to save the time **up to your last set** instead.

### Tabata timer

An interval timer for HIIT. Open it from the side menu (**Tabata Timer**) or from a Tabata exercise in a program (**Launch Tabata Timer**).

- A ring drains over each part: **Get ready** (amber), **Work** (your accent colour) and **Rest** (blue). Round bars show how many rounds are done, and it tells you what is **next** and how much time is **left**.
- **Pause / Resume**, **start over** (left button) and **skip** to the next part (right button). When all rounds are done, **Go again** runs it once more.
- The sliders button at the top changes the timing: **work, rest, rounds and get-ready time**, or a preset (Classic 20/10 ×8, 30/15, 40/20, 45/15, 30/30). Rest can be 0.
- Opened from the side menu, it remembers your last timing. A program's own Tabata exercise uses the program's numbers, and changes there apply to that run only.
- It beeps at 3, 2, 1 and at each change (following your Sound and Vibrate settings) and keeps the screen on, on phones that allow it. It times itself from the clock, so if your phone sleeps for a bit, it catches up to the right place when you return.
- The layout leaves room for the notch and home bar. That spacing is done the standard way but has not been tried on a real iPhone.

### Plate calculator

Shows which plates to load on each side, including gym plates. It is also a tile on Home.

### 1-rep-max (1RM) calculator

Home → **1RM Calculator**. Type a hard set (for example 225 × 5) and it estimates your one-rep max (262.5). Below is a chart from **100% down to 50%**: the weight at each percent and about how many reps that is.

- **Round to the plates I have** (on by default) rounds every weight to what your bar and plates can really load, using the plates from Settings.
- **Tap any row** to open the plate calculator at that weight.
- **Or start from one of my records…** fills in a lift you already have a record for.
- It uses the same Epley formula as your records, so the numbers match everywhere. It also shows Brzycki and Lombardi for comparison, and warns that estimates get less accurate above about 12 reps.
- It remembers your last numbers and follows your kg / lb setting.

### Your own exercises

Missing a lift? Add it from the **Create your own exercise** button in the exercise picker (searching for something that doesn't exist offers it with that name), from the **＋ Create your own exercise** button in the Exercise Library, or from **My Exercises** on Home.

- **Pick the main muscle** (14 choices). It counts toward your weekly muscle volume and lights up on the muscle map like any built-in exercise.
- **Pick how you log it:** weight × reps, dumbbells, bodyweight ± weight, cardio with distance, or timed.
- Your exercise works everywhere: workouts, programs, charts, records, history and swaps.
- **Home → My Exercises** lists them. The name is locked after you create it so your history keeps matching. Deleting asks first.
- They are saved in your settings, so backups include them.

## Progress and records

The **Progress** screen has three tabs: **Overview**, **Muscles** and **Strength**.

### Overview

- **Streak, this week and total workouts** at a glance.
- **Plateau check:** lifts that haven't improved in your last 3 sessions, each with a tip (below).
- **Monthly recap:** a card summarising your month (workouts, volume, top muscles, PRs) that you can share as an image. A banner shows on Home in the first 10 days of each month.
- **Lifetime totals:** total volume, total sets, and the month you started.

### Muscles

- **Weekly sets per muscle:** how many hard sets each muscle got (warm-ups left out). Switch between **This week**, **Last week** and **4-wk avg**. **½ Helpers** counts assisting muscles as half a set.
- **Targets:** each muscle has a target range, the grey zone on its bar. The bar is green inside the range, orange below it and red above it, and the map colours match. Starting ranges come from common training-volume guidelines (for example chest 8–20 sets a week). Tap any muscle to set your own, or **Default** to reset it.
- **Recovery map:** tap **Recovery** at the top of the card. The map turns red (just trained), amber (recovering) or green (ready), and the list shows how long each muscle still needs. It is an estimate based on how many hard sets each muscle got and how long ago, giving bigger muscles and harder sessions more time (about 1 to 3.5 days).
- **Muscle map:** a detailed front and back body with 40+ muscles. Tap one to see its sets, or how recovered it is.
- **Balance check:** compares your last 4 weeks: push vs pull, quads vs hamstrings + glutes, and upper vs lower body. If one side is far ahead it says so, with a simple fix (for example "add rows or pulldowns"). It needs about 12 sets of data first. A mismatch isn't automatically bad; it is worth knowing about.

### Strength

- **Strength level:** Beginner → Novice → Intermediate → Advanced → Elite for bench, squat, deadlift and overhead press, based on StrengthLevel.com standards (men and women), plus the **1,000 lb club** total.
- **Recent PRs:** your 5 latest records, with **Show all** for the rest.

### Plateau tips

A lift counts as **stalled** when its estimated max hasn't beaten your best in your last 3 sessions. The app then works out why and suggests something that fits:

| What happened | Tip |
|---|---|
| Same weight 3 sessions in a row | Add a rep to every set before adding weight, or take a smaller jump (2.5 lb / 1 kg). |
| Reps climbing at the same weight | Keep going. Add weight when you hit the top of your rep range. |
| Added weight but lost reps | Stay at that weight until the reps come back. |
| Down 5% or more from your best | Check sleep and food, and consider a lighter week. |
| Anything else | Try a small weight drop, a different rep range, or a similar exercise for a few weeks. |

The tip shows on the Overview tab, on the exercise card in your workout, and on the exercise's chart screen.

### Records and history

- **Personal records:** new records for weight, estimated 1-rep max and volume appear in the **Trophy Room** on Home.
- **Per-exercise charts:** weight, estimated max and rep records over time.
- **Streaks and weekly goal:** set how many days a week you want to train and keep your streak alive.
- **Up next on Home** includes a line about today's muscles, like "Chest, Shoulders still recovering (ready in about 3 days)" or "✓ Muscles are rested for this day".
- **Calendar and History:** see every past workout. Open one to edit it, **Do Again**, **Share** it, or **Save as Routine**. Tap the **Time** box on a past workout to fix its length.

## Body tracking

- **Weight trend:** each weigh-in is a dot, with a smooth 7-day average line so water and food swings don't fool you.
- **Trend, Pace and Goal:** under the chart you see your trend weight, your pace (lb or kg per week, from the last 4 weeks) and your goal. Tap **Goal** to set one. It is drawn as a dashed line, with how far you have left and about how many weeks that takes at your current pace. Pace needs weigh-ins spanning about a week.
- **Measurements:** chest, waist, arms, legs and more, with charts. Bodyweight and body fat come first in the log; the rest is under **More: measurements & nutrition**.
- **Progress photos:** stored privately on your phone, never uploaded. There is no limit on how many you keep.

## Backup and sharing

> 💡 **Back up now and then.** Everything lives on your phone. If you clear browser data or get a new phone, a backup file is how you keep it all.

- **Backup and Restore:** save all your data to a file, and load it back on this phone or a new one.
- **No storage cap:** when the normal space fills up, PulseTrack keeps saving in your phone's large storage, for as long as your phone has room. Settings shows **No size limit**.
- **CSV export:** download your workout history for Excel or Google Sheets.
- **Share a program** as a link, or **import** one.
- **Share a workout:** after you finish, or from **History → a workout → Share**, you get a picture card (date, time, volume, sets, PRs, the muscles you worked and your best set for each lift, with a gold PR badge and how your estimated max changed). It uses your theme colours and font. **Share image** sends it to Messages, Instagram and so on, **Copy as text** pastes it into any chat, and **Save image to this phone** keeps it.

## Settings and looks

Settings has six groups: **Workout**, **Sound, Voice & Feel**, **Appearance**, **Data & Backup**, **App** and **Danger Zone**. Tap one to open it; each shows a short summary of your choices, and the app remembers which were open until you close it.

- **Units:** kg or lb. Switching asks whether to **Convert my saved numbers**, **Only change the labels**, or **Cancel**.
- **Text size:** make everything bigger or smaller.
- **Auto-start Rest Timer:** on or off. The rest timer also has your own times, sounds and voice.
- **Strength level:** men's or women's standards.
- **Weekly goal:** days per week.
- **Home screen:** pick which cards show on Home and in what order.

### Appearance

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | 13 one-tap looks: nine dark (Classic, Midnight, Crimson, Neon, Sunset, Royal, Ocean, Forest, Iron) and four light (Paper, Daylight, Sandy, Bloom). A theme sets mode, colour, background, effect and font together; change any one afterwards and the rest stays. |
| **Mode** | **Dark**, **Light**, or **Auto** (follows your phone and switches live). |
| **Accent colour** | 18 ready-made colours, or the rainbow circle for your own: type a **hex code** like `#FF3366` or drag the three sliders. Colours are adjusted per mode so text stays readable. |
| **Background** | Dark: Classic, True black, Midnight, Slate, Forest, Plum, Ember, Wine. Light: Paper, Snow, Cloud, Sand, Mint, Blush, Lavender, Stone. Or a **custom hex** for either (adjusted to suit the mode). Dark and light remember their own. |
| **Background effect** | **Solid**, **Glow**, **Fade**, **Aurora**, or **Gradient** (two colours of your own). |
| **Font** | System, Poppins, Outfit, Work Sans, Big Shoulders, Tektur, Lora, JetBrains Mono. They are built in, so they work offline. |
| **Corners** | **Sharp**, **Normal** or **Round**. |
| **Row size** | **Compact**, **Normal** or **Roomy** for set rows and check buttons. Roomy is easier with sweaty hands. |

**Reset look** puts colours, background, effect, font and corners back to the original. It does not touch your Home layout, your own exercises or your data.

**Home layout** (Settings → Appearance → Home screen): use ▲ ▼ to move a card and **Shown / Hidden** to hide it. The cards are the monthly recap banner, today's workout, shortcut tiles, consistency, and the 1RM Trophy Room. **Put the cards back the way they were** resets it.

App pop-ups (confirmations, text boxes, lists) match your theme in light or dark, and their buttons say what they do, like **Remove**.

## Good to know

**Works offline.** After the first load, everything (programs, exercises, charts, styling, fonts) is stored in the app. Only the YouTube "How to do it" links and getting updates need internet.

**iPhone**
- Updates can lag. Close the app fully and reopen it online.
- If you don't use the app for a long time, iPhone may clear its stored files. Keep a backup.
- It has not been tested on a real iPhone.

**About the numbers.** Recovery times and target ranges are estimates and typical guidelines, not medical or coaching advice. Form tips are general guidance: if something hurts, stop and ask a coach or doctor.

**What a web app can't do.** Apple Health or Strava sync, a watch app, home-screen widgets, a lock-screen timer, or reminder notifications. Those need a native app or a server.

**About the 12-week programs.** The source plans had a few gaps, handled like this:
- Ferrigno: the heading says weeks 5–8, but the schedule says weeks 7–12.
- Arnold: no phase lengths were given, so each phase is 3 weeks.
- Mountain Dog: one week repeats for 12 weeks.
- Phil Heath Phase 3: cardio has no time listed.
- Ronnie Coleman: crunches have no sets or reps listed.

## Managing the project

### Files

Upload these to your GitHub repo, all in the same folder:

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The whole app (about 905 KB, including fonts, themes and form tips) |
| `programs.js` | The 21 built-in programs |
| `exercises.js` | The 474 exercises |
| `sw.js` | Makes it work offline and update |
| `manifest.json` | Lets it install to your home screen |
| `icon-512.png` | The app icon |
| `README.md` | This file |

**Update steps:** upload the changed files on GitHub → open the app online → close and reopen.

### How program changes reach phones

- A program that is **new** in `programs.js` shows up on phones automatically.
- A program that **already existed** and was edited is *not* replaced on phones, so your progress is safe. To get the changed version, delete the program in the app and use **Add Back a Built-in Program**.
- The one exception is wording: if a built-in program still carries an old source note, the app swaps in the cleaned text by itself. It only touches text containing an old note, so anything you wrote or edited is never changed, and no exercise, set, rep or logged workout changes.

### Adding programs and exercises

- **Easiest:** build a program inside the app, or import a `.json` file with the Import button.
- **Permanent for everyone:** add it to `programs.js`, one object per program with `id`, `title`, `description`, `tags` and `days`. Each day has `dayNumber`, `title`, `estimatedMinutes` and `exercises` (each with `id`, `name`, `sets`, `reps`, `restSeconds`, `type`, `notes`). For a 12-week program, add `week: 1…12` to every day. An empty `exercises` list is a rest day.
- **New exercises:** use **My Exercises** in the app, or add them to `exercises.js` for everyone.

Keep every `id` unique. New programs reach phones on the next update.
