# PulseTrack 💪

**A free workout tracker that lives on your phone and works with no internet.**
Pick a program, log your sets, beat your records. No account, no ads, no sign-up. Your data stays on your phone.

*Current version: 2.3.0*

---

## In one line

PulseTrack is a gym log app you install on your home screen. It has 21 ready-made programs (including 12-week plans from Arnold, Ronnie Coleman, Phil Heath and more), 474 exercises, rest timers, charts, and records, and it works in the gym with zero signal.

## 1. Install it

Open the app link in your phone's browser once (with internet). Then:

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap the Share button → **Add to Home Screen** → Add.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the ⋮ menu → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

After that it opens like a normal app, full screen, and works offline.

---

## 2. How to update it

When new files are uploaded to GitHub:

1. Open the app **with internet**.
2. Close it fully and open it again (or refresh).
3. That's it. You get the new version and your data stays.

**Android** updates on the first refresh. **iPhone** can be slower, so if you don't see the update, close the app completely and open it again while online.

---

## 3. Your first workout

1. Open the app. You land on **Home**.
2. Tap **Programs** and choose one (or start **Freestyle** for a free workout).
3. Tap a day. You see every exercise with its sets, reps and rest.
4. For each set, type your **weight** and **reps**, then tap the check mark.
5. A rest timer starts by itself.
6. Tap **Finish Workout** at the end. You get a summary and the next day lines up as **Up next**.

### 12-week program features
- Every day is labelled like **Week 4 of 12 · Day 2**.
- The days list has week headers (Week 1 … Week 12) and scrolls to **Up next** for you.
- **Jump to week** lets you start in the middle. Earlier weeks are marked done.
- **Rest days** are skipped automatically. You never have to tap them.
- History remembers which week each workout was in.

### Make your own
- **Program builder:** create a program, add days, add exercises, set sets, reps and rest.
- **Edit any program:** add, remove, swap or reorder exercises, change sets and reps.
- **Save a finished workout as a routine** to repeat it.
- **Supersets** are supported (shown as A1/A2, B1/B2).
- **Import** a program from a `.json` file, or from a **share link** a friend sends you.
- **Share** any program as a link.

### Reset and restore
- **Clear Progress** puts a day back to exactly the program's original (removes added exercises and extra sets, brings back removed ones). **Your History and records are never deleted.**
- **Deleted a built-in program?** Use **Add Back a Built-in Program** to get it back.

## 5. While you train

- **Set tags:** mark a set as **R** (regular), **W** (warm-up), **D** (drop set) or **F** (failure).
- **Smart rest timer:** rest time adapts to the exercise (heavy lifts get longer, small lifts shorter). You can set your own time for any exercise. Sound, vibration and optional voice tell you when rest is over.
- **Previous numbers:** each set shows what you did last time so you know what to beat.
- **Plate calculator:** tells you which plates to load on each side (includes gym plates).
- **Warm-up generator:** builds warm-up sets for your first lift.
- **Bodyweight exercises:** pull-ups and dips use your body weight, with a **Weighted / Assisted** switch.
- **Dumbbells:** enter the weight of **one** dumbbell. The app counts both hands in your volume.
- **Cardio:** log **minutes and distance**, and see your pace over time.
- **Tabata timer:** built-in interval timer for HIIT.
- **Swap an exercise:** tap swap and pick a similar one. **★ Best swaps** shows the closest matches first.
- **How to do it:** opens a YouTube search for that exercise's form.
- **Notes:** add a note to any exercise or any day.
- **Screen stays awake** during a workout (if your phone allows it).
- **Resume:** if you leave the app mid-workout, it picks up where you stopped.

## 6. Progress and records

- **Personal records (PRs):** the app spots new records for weight, estimated 1-rep max and volume, and shows them in the **Trophy Room**.
- **Per-exercise charts:** see weight, estimated max and rep records over time.
- **Weekly muscle volume:** how many hard sets each muscle got this week. A **½ Helpers** option counts helper muscles as half a set.
- **Muscle map:** front and back body picture that lights up trained muscles. Tap a muscle to see its sets.
- **Strength level:** Beginner → Novice → Intermediate → Advanced → Elite for bench, squat, deadlift and overhead press, based on StrengthLevel.com standards (men and women). Includes the **1,000 lb club** total.
- **Streaks and weekly goal:** set how many days a week you want to train and keep your streak alive.
- **Calendar and History:** see every past workout. You can open one, edit it, **Do Again**, or **Save as Routine**.
- **Monthly recap:** a summary card of your month (workouts, volume, top muscles, PRs). Share it as an image. A banner shows on Home in the first 10 days of each month.

## 8. Backup and sharing

- **Backup:** save all your data to a file. **Restore** loads it back on this phone or a new one.
- **CSV export:** download your workout history to open in Excel or Google Sheets.
- **Share a program** as a link, or **import** one.

> 💡 **Back up now and then.** Everything lives on your phone. If you clear browser data or get a new phone, a backup file is how you keep it all.

## 9. Settings

- **Units:** kg or lb. Switching converts your numbers.
- **Theme:** several colour themes.

- **Rest timer:** auto, smart or your own times; sounds and voice on or off.

## 10. Works offline

After the first load, PulseTrack needs **no internet**. Everything (programs, exercises, charts, styling) is stored in the app. The only things that need internet are the YouTube "How to do it" link and getting updates.

## 11. Good to know

**iPhone**
- Updates can lag. Close the app fully and reopen it online.
- If you don't use the app for a long time, iPhone may clear its stored files. **Keep a backup.**
- This has not been tested on a real iPhone.

**What a web app can't do**
- Apple Health or Strava sync, a watch app, home-screen widgets, a lock-screen timer, or reminder notifications. These need a native app or a server.

**Programs and updates**
- A program that is **new** in `programs.js` shows up on phones automatically.
- A program that **already existed** and was edited is *not* replaced on phones. To get the changed version: delete the program in the app, then use **Add Back a Built-in Program**.
- The old split versions of the 12-week programs were removed automatically.

**Numbers changed once in version 2.2**
- Volume was recalculated one time (dumbbells count ×2, pull-ups and dips use body weight). Old totals may look different. This is expected.

**Gaps in the source PDFs for the 12-week programs**
- Ferrigno: the heading says weeks 5–8, but the schedule says weeks 7–12.
- Arnold: the PDF gives no phase lengths, so each phase is 3 weeks.
- Mountain Dog: one week repeats.
- Phil Heath Phase 3: cardio has no time listed.
- Ronnie Coleman: crunches have no sets or reps listed.

## 13. Adding your own programs and exercises

- **Easiest:** build a program inside the app, or import a `.json` file using the Import button.
- **Permanent for everyone:** add it to `programs.js` (one object per program: `id`, `title`, `description`, `tags`, `days`). Each day has `dayNumber`, `title`, `estimatedMinutes` and `exercises` (each with `id`, `name`, `sets`, `reps`, `restSeconds`, `type`, `notes`). For a 12-week program, add `week: 1…12` to every day. An empty `exercises` list is a rest day.
- **New exercises:** add them to `exercises.js`.

Keep every `id` unique. New programs reach phones on the next update.
